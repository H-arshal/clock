12. ⏱️ Stopwatch Timing Logic

This is important.

❌ Don't rely entirely on:
setInterval(() => {
    seconds++;
}, 1000);

Because browser timers aren't perfectly accurate.

Instead use timestamps.

Conceptually:

startTime
    ↓
currentTime
    ↓
elapsedTime = currentTime - startTime

Use:

performance.now()

for accurate elapsed timing.

Then calculate:

milliseconds
seconds
minutes
hours

from elapsed milliseconds.
